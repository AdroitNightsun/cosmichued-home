## 🌷 Welcome In!

Life is messy, so let's make it a little lovely. **cosmichued** is a dreamy, frosted-glass digital sanctuary designed to feel like a warm cup of tea on a rainy afternoon — no pressure, no perfect, just a safe place to land and linger.

### ✨ What We Talk About

* 📖 **Bookish Days** — Cozy reads, favorite quotes, dog-eared pages, and the stories that stay with us long after the final chapter.
* 🌸 **Tiny Joys** — The small, soft things: golden-hour light across the floor, that first sip of morning coffee, and little rituals that make an ordinary Tuesday special.
* 🦋 **Honest Thoughts** — Navigating life in our twenties and beyond: late-night ponderings, messy milestones, and learning to give ourselves grace along the way.

---

## 🎀 Aesthetic & Features

* **☁️ Pink Frosted Glassmorphism**: Translucent backdrop blurs, pastel gradients, and delicate borders inspired by soft moodboards.
* **☀️ Day / 🌙 Night Switch**: Seamlessly transition from a sunlit blush fog to a midnight lilac sky complete with twinkling constellations and shooting stars.
* **🌸 Swaying Petals**: Hand-drawn SVG flowers that drift up and sway gently across the page as you scroll.
* **🫧 Buttery Smooth Scroll**: Powered by Lenis for fluid navigation, paired with subtle photo parallax and a progress indicator.
* **✨ Shimmering Hero**: Animated glowing typography that captures the light.
* **📱 Fully Responsive**: Thoughtfully tuned for phones, tablets, and wide screens alike.

## 💻 Local Exploration

Want to run this space locally on your own machine?

1. **Clone the repository:**
   ```bash
   git clone https://github.com/AdroitNightsun/cosmichued-home.git
   cd cosmichued-home
   ```

2. **Open in browser:**
   Double-click `index.html` or serve it with any lightweight server:
   ```bash
   # Using Python
   python3 -m http.server 8000
   ```
   Then visit `http://localhost:8000` in your favorite browser! 🌷
